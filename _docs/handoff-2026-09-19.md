## The decision behind it

The scribble pad sat on the **Wound up** face of the feeling picker. It told a
furious person to "scribble as hard as you like". That is an arousal-raising
activity, and the anger evidence runs one way on this.

Kjærvik & Bushman, Clinical Psychology Review, 2024. A meta-analysis of roughly
154 studies, around 10,000 participants. Arousal-**lowering** activities
reduced anger. Arousal-**raising** activities did not, and some increased it.
Hitting things, venting and jogging are all in the second group.

So two moves were agreed:

| Screen | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wound up (picker) | Scribble pad | A tighten-and-stop script — **not this task** |
| Play button (Home) | Does nothing | The scribble pad |

The scribble pad is **not** being deleted. Opened from Home by somebody who is
not angry, a fast scribble is just drawing, and the fade is charm rather than
therapy. That is what fixes the evidence problem instead of dodging it.

The Wound up replacement is specified in
`_docs/briefs/wound-up-tighten-and-stop.md`. **Do not build it in this task.**
The user is still reading the script aloud with a timer.

## How to check it

```
flutter analyze
flutter test
```

Then run the app and do this by hand:

1. Home → tap **Play** → the pad opens.
2. Draw. The mark holds, fades, and is gone with nothing pressed.
3. Tap **I'm done** → back on Home.
4. Open it again → tap the **X** → back on Home.
5. Home → **Tap me** → picker → tap **Wound up** → nothing happens, and
   nothing crashes.

Step 5 is the one most likely to be missed. Wound up is now a dead face on
purpose, and it must fail quietly.
