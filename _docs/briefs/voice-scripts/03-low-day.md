# 03 — A low day: somebody else, and you too

About **7 minutes** once the app puts the gaps in. Your recording will be much
shorter than that, because you do not record the silences.

The person hearing this is having a low day. Flat, sad, hard on themselves.

## What happens

They think of someone they care about and say kind words for that person.
Then they put a hand on their own chest. Then the same kind words include
them too.

## Important — every line is its own file

This is **not** one long recording, and we do not cut it up afterwards.

Every line below has a label like **A1** or **D4**. Each label is one take and
one file. Record them one at a time, in order, and stop between them.

We tried the other way on the panic script. The reader gave us one performance,
we cut it into pieces, and the cuts landed in the wrong places. This avoids all
of it.

What that means for you:

- **The `[PAUSE]` lines are not recorded.** They are not silence you leave in.
  They are the app holding the screen still. You do not have to time anything.
- **A fluffed line costs one line.** Say it again and we keep the better take.
  You never re-run a part.
- **Do not leave silence at the start of a take.** The app plays the file the
  moment the words go on screen, so leading silence looks like a broken app.
- **Leave about half a second at the end** of every take.

## The two kind lines are recorded ONCE

**K1** and **K2** are the heart of this script. They are heard **four times** —
twice for the other person, twice for the listener.

You record them **once each**. The app plays the same two files both times.

That is the whole answer to the note that used to sit at the top of this page:
"say them exactly the same way all four times." Now they cannot be different.
They are the same recording.

So take your time on those two. They are the two most important takes in the
script.

## The voice for this script

- Soft and level. Slower than normal speech, but not sleepy.
- Warm, but never bright. No lift, no encouragement.
- Company, not comfort. You are sitting beside them, not looking after them.

## Do not

- Do not get emotional. Some people cry at this script. Your steadiness is
  what holds it.
- Do not sound hopeful. Nothing here promises tomorrow will be better.
- Do not stress "you" in "and they are for you as well". Say it plainly.
- Do not sound like you are giving a gift.

## Staying the same across 38 takes

This is the one risk of recording line by line. Over 38 takes a voice drifts.

- Read the whole script through once out loud before you record anything. Get
  the pace into your body.
- Then record in order, front to back, in **one sitting**.
- Do not move the mic, change the room, or change your distance part way.
- If you have to stop and come back, tell us where you stopped.

## How to read the pauses

`[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]` is **not spoken and not recorded**. It tells us how long
the app holds that screen before the next line arrives. It is here so you can
see the shape of the script.

Between two lines with no `[PAUSE]` between them, the app leaves a short gap on
its own. You do not record that either.

# PART A — Settling

**A1**

Find a position you could stay in for a while.

**A2**

Let your spine be long, and let your shoulders drop.

**A3**

Rest your hands wherever they land.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**A4**

You can stop whenever you want. Nothing here has to be finished.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**A5**

Your eyes can close, or stay open and go loose.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**A6**

It is a low day.

**A7**

You do not have to explain it to anyone.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

Note on A6: "It is a low day." is a plain fact. Not sad, not sympathetic. Just
true.

Note on A1 to A3: these work standing, sitting or lying down, and the script
never asks which. Do not add a word that picks one, and do not read them as if
the person is in a chair.

---

# PART B — Held

**B1**

Feel whatever is under you, holding your weight.

**B2**

It is doing all the work.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**B3**

Let yourself be heavy on it.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**B4**

Nothing has to be lifted right now.

[PAUSE 12 SECONDS]

---

# PART C — Somebody else

**C1**

Now let somebody you care about come to mind.

**C2**

Whoever turns up first. A person, or an animal.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**C3**

They are somewhere else right now, having their own day.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**C4**

Some plain words for them. There is nothing to answer.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**K1** — first of two plays

I hope today is easy.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**K2** — first of two plays

I hope you are alright.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**C5**

You are allowed to feel exactly what you feel.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

Note on K1 and K2: say them simply, the way you would say them to a friend on
the phone. Not solemn. Not a blessing.

---

# PART D — Your own hand

**D1**

Now bring one hand up, and rest it on the middle of your chest.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**D2**

Let it sit there with some weight.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**D3**

Feel the warmth of your palm coming through.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

**D4**

This is your own hand.

**D5**

It has been warm all day.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

Note on D5: "It has been warm all day" is almost a small aside. Quiet and
ordinary.

---

# PART E — And you too

**E1**

Now somebody who has had this feeling all day.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**E2**

That is you.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**E3**

The same words again, and they are for you as well.

[PAUSE 5 SECONDS]

**K1** — second play. Not recorded again.

I hope today is easy.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

**K2** — second play. Not recorded again.

I hope you are alright.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

**E4**

You are allowed to feel exactly this much.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

**E5**

Right now, somebody else is feeling exactly this.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

**E6**

It is a human way to feel.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

Note on E2: "That is you." is the turn of the whole script. Say it gently and
without weight. No pause for effect before it — the silence after it does the
work.

---

# PART F — Wider

**F1**

Now let your hand come down, and rest it wherever it lands.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**F2**

Feel the air on your face.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**F3**

Feel the weight of your feet.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**F4**

Feel how much room there is around you.

[PAUSE 10 SECONDS]

Note on F3: "the weight of your feet" is true standing, sitting or lying down.
It is not "your feet on the floor" any more, so do not read it that way.

---

# PART G — Leaving

**G1**

Your feet are heavy, and your hands are resting.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**G2**

This is how it is today.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**G3**

Stay as long as you want.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**G4**

Nothing is finished, and nothing has to be.

[PAUSE 8 SECONDS]

**G5**

Your hand can go back there whenever you want.

Note on G5: this is an offer. Light. Then stop. Do not warm up the ending.

---

# What is never spoken

| On screen | Why it is silent |
| --- | --- |
| The X, and any button label | A voice reading a button is the app talking about itself. |
| The screen title, if there is one | It is a label, not part of the script. |

---

**If a line changes, its take changes with it.** A screen whose words and voice
differ is worse than a slightly clumsy line: the person is trying to follow one
thing and is handed two. Changing a line here means re-recording that one take —
or not changing the line.

Because every line is its own file, that now costs one take instead of a whole
session. That is the main reason this script is built this way.
