Sidekick — Moss & cream

Theme 3C · MVP · every screen, both modes

Every Moss & cream screen decided so far, in one place. Home, the breathing step of the panic flow, and Settings. Component specs and the token table live in Sidekick UI Kit.

The theme's one rule worth remembering: action is two values. Deep moss #3D5232 in light, gold #D9A640 in dark, because moss disappears against the dark canvas. The panic button is #C2542A in both.

HOME NOTES

The scene gradient is the only place the theme goes full-bleed. Everything under it sits on the canvas at 20px gutters.

Meditate and Play are derived from the primary button: a paler cut of it as fill, labelled in the action colour. They are never the action colour themselves.

List cards are the lightest surface in the theme — pure white in light, a lifted moss step in dark.

BREATHING NOTES

Close and mute are the only chrome. Both are 52px circles on a 12% ink wash, mirrored so close sits under the left thumb.

Next uses the same two action values as Home. Dark gold on dark moss is the higher-contrast pair of the two, which suits the screen a user reaches while panicking.

Settings

The chrome-heavy screen: grouped lists, 56pt rows, toggles, values, a segmented picker, theme swatches and a destructive row. Two frames at the top of the list, one scrolled to the end.

SETTINGS NOTES

Toggles are the panic orange when on, in both modes. It's the one control where the app's most urgent colour also means "yes, this is on", and it stays legible on every surface.

Row dividers use the hairline slot (ECE7D4 / 47593E), not the card border. Card borders at row scale read as a table.

Baloo 2 is display only — large title, the sidekick's name, card titles. Every setting row label is Nunito 17px.

STILL OPEN

Delete (B3311F) and the panic FAB (C2542A) share the scrolled screen and nearly a hue. Resolved by pushing delete darker and redder, but worth a second look on a real device.

"Someone to call" is doing crisis work from inside a settings list. It may deserve its own screen.
